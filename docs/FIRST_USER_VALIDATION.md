# First-user validation session

Status: prepared, not conducted. Do not treat this document as user feedback or evidence of customer demand.

Purpose: see whether a finance learner or instructor can understand a bank-shock investigation without the builder narrating the interface. Keep Parallax’s existing task: investigate bank shocks, credit, and rescue comparisons.

Ask permission to take notes. Use an unfamiliar participant, a fresh browser session and the same identified build. Record the participant’s relevant background without unnecessary personal information. Record the input sample, scenario, settlement version, seed, horizon and chosen loss so another person can reproduce the task.

Give these prompts one at a time:

1. Explain what you think this product does from its introduction.
2. Create an economy and apply a 40% investment loss. Before continuing, tell me what you think the 40% refers to.
3. Follow consequences. Tell me when you think the observation has finished, and describe what changed.
4. Find one business that was affected. Explain what happened to its credit, payroll or jobs and what evidence supports that explanation.
5. Compare with no additional loss. Does the screen establish that every lost job was caused by the shock? Explain your answer.
6. Ask the live agents to investigate. Identify what the investigator, planner and supervisor contributed. Explain whether a recommendation has already moved money.
7. Explore rescue policies. Explain the difference between comparing a response and applying it.

Do not teach the answer during the task. If the participant gets stuck, record the point of confusion and the assistance supplied. Record delays and provider errors as they occur. Do not repeat only to obtain favorable results.

For each task record: completed unaided / completed with help / not completed; the participant’s explanation; the control or label they searched for; factual misunderstandings; observed wait; requested assistance. Ask which part was useful and what they would use it for. Avoid implying that they should praise the project.

Interpretation checklist:

- Did they understand the loss is on one bank’s risky assets rather than the entire economy?
- Could they identify the starting employment denominator and the final percentage?
- Did they distinguish recorded sequence from causal proof?
- Could they identify bank-side constraints separately from borrower weakness?
- Did they notice completion and find alternate worlds?
- Did they distinguish model calculations from AI investigation?
- Did they understand supervision can hold a result and that applying support is separate?

After the session, fix material observed confusion, preserve the initial notes, and repeat with another unfamiliar person if available. A few usability observations do not validate real lending decisions, economic accuracy or market demand. Report actual participant count and observations only.
